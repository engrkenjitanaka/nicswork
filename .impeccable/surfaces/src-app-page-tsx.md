## Direction contract

THESIS: A reel, not a résumé. The page is a dark screening room where Jonji's
own footage and artwork are the only bright things; it refuses the freelancer-
landing-page arrangement of skills grid, stat row, and testimonial wall. The
incumbent Canva identity is inherited, not replaced: same black, same portrait,
same tracked capitals — rebuilt so the work drives rather than a fixed canvas.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground (#08090A) with panels lifted one step (#101113).
Warm amber (#E0A95F), sampled from the practical LEDs in the hero portrait,
used only where the visitor acts: focus ring, active section marker, copy
confirmation. Poppins throughout — geometric, wide-tracked capitals for section
and category labels, tight negative tracking on display sizes. Work sits on the
ground with no card chrome; edges come from the artwork's own alpha, not borders.

STORY: A prospective client lands, sees a designer at his desk in warm low light,
scrolls into work that plays, and leaves with his email in their clipboard.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed portrait, warm light right, subject right of centre.
Signature script "Hello, I'm" sits above the name at the left third; "Jonji Jose
C." runs as the largest type on the page, capped at 6rem, tracking -0.04em.
Rule, then wide-tracked "MULTIMEDIA DESIGNER", then the disciplines. Intro sits
in a bordered plate at lower left with "Contact Me" beneath it. Nav pill floats
top, its active marker warm. The name assembles once on load: the only
non-user-triggered motion above the fold.

FORM: Single scrolling page, structure inherited from the incumbent, refined
into a real responsive grid. Precisely specified request within an established
world — no concept tournament, by new-work.md section 3. Seed key: none.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
