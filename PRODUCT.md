# Jonji Jose Coronel — Portfolio

## What this is

A personal portfolio for Jonji Jose Coronel, a multimedia designer based in the
Philippines working across graphic design and video editing. One page. Its whole
job is to let someone decide, quickly, that he is worth hiring — and then reach
him.

Replaces an existing Canva-published site at nicswork.online. Same person, same
work, same content; rebuilt so the work is not held back by the export.

## Who it is for

Prospective clients evaluating a freelance multimedia designer:

- Real-estate agents and brokerages needing listing and success-story video
- Brands and businesses needing short-form social content, UGC, and testimonials
- Coaches, podcasters, and educators needing long-form and repurposed video
- Businesses needing brand kits, magazines, decks, and marketing collateral

They arrive from a link in a message, a proposal, or a bio — usually on a phone,
usually giving it under a minute before deciding to keep scrolling or leave.

## What it must prove

That the work is good. Not that he says it is. The videos and the design work
are the argument; everything else on the page gets out of their way.
